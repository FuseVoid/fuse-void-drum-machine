Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 19, 2026

Fuse Void MK1 is an offline-first professional drum machine for iOS. We built it so your patterns, samples, and creativity stay on your device — not on our servers.

Summary

We do not collect personal data. We do not use analytics, advertising trackers, or cookies. We do not sell data. The app works without an account and without a constant internet connection.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information from you. Fuse Void does not require sign-in, profile creation, or cloud sync to operate.

We do not receive your name, email, location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, or usage analytics through the app.

Data stored on your device

The following stays locally on your iPad or iPhone unless you choose to export it:

  • Patterns, sequences, and slot data you create in the app
  • Mixer, FX, and performance settings during your session
  • MIDI files you export using the built-in export feature

You control this data. Deleting the app removes app-local storage from your device, subject to iOS system behavior.

No tracking & no third-party sharing

Fuse Void does not use third-party analytics, crash-reporting SDKs, ad networks, or social tracking pixels. This website does not use cookies for tracking or profiling.

Website

This marketing site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Standard web server logs (such as IP address and browser type) may be processed by the hosting provider for security and delivery. We do not use those logs to identify or profile visitors.

App Store & purchases

If you purchase Fuse Void on the App Store, payment and receipt handling is managed entirely by Apple. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple's privacy policy applies to that transaction: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Children's privacy

Fuse Void is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page if the app or legal requirements change. The date at the top of this page will be revised when that happens. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
